{{icon "info"}}Using a drive in Steam
  1. Add the drive here. VaporOS mounts it at /var/mnt/<name> on every start and adds it to Steam's libraries, right away or the next time Steam isn't running.
  2. To use it before it shows Added to Steam, stream Steam with Moonlight and open Settings → Storage.
  3. Choose Add drive and pick the folder shown here. Games already on it appear after Steam scans it.

Drives formatted as exFAT or FAT can't hold Steam games. Use ext4, btrfs, xfs, f2fs or NTFS.
