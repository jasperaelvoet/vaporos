Extensions add software that isn't part of VaporOS. Each one is a sealed, read-only package that updates and rolls back together with VaporOS, and its settings and downloads are kept apart. Removing one removes its files; its card lists anything it changed in Steam or downloaded.

Restart to finish

VaporOS restarts by itself when nobody is playing.

If VaporOS doesn't start

When an extension keeps VaporOS from starting, it goes back by itself. You can also leave every extension out of the next start.
